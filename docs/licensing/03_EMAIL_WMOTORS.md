**Subject:** Game Licensing Enquiry — Lykan Hypersport / Fenyr SuperSport for an Indie Arcade Racer (PC)

---

Dear W Motors team,

My name is [YOUR NAME] — I am the founder of **[STUDIO NAME]**, a small independent studio making an original arcade racing game for PC. I am writing to ask whether W Motors would be open to licensing one of your hypercars for it.

I want to say at the outset that the idea came from something I genuinely admire: the Lykan Hypersport's appearance in *Furious 7* is a large part of why hypercars look the way they do in games, and I have been following W Motors' own cars — the Lykan, the Fenyr SuperSport and the Sabre — ever since.

**Why I am writing to you specifically.** W Motors built its reputation on cars that are instantly recognisable from a single silhouette, in a country with no indigenous automotive heritage of its own. That is a difficult thing to engineer, and it is exactly the quality a game garage needs: a car a player can identify from a thumbnail, in a menu, at 200 metres, without a badge.

**The project.** *[STUDIO NAME]* is an original arcade racer built in Unity (HDRP) for Windows PC. It is an original title — the genre's accessible design language (a visible garage of switchable cars, drift, a nitro boost, three AI rivals, short two-lap circuit races, instant restarts) with no third-party code, art, audio or content. Players pick a car, see it on a rotating garage turntable, and race that same car on a golden-hour circuit. The showroom car and the race car are the same asset, so what appears in marketing is exactly what the player drives.

**Where the car would appear.** As a player-selectable car and as an AI opponent, on the garage turntable, and — subject to your approval — on the store page, in trailers and in a press kit. We would not publish any image of the car before written approval of the depiction, and we would not make any performance, pricing or availability claim about the real vehicle.

**What I am asking for.** A **single car** to start — ideally the **Lykan Hypersport**, because it is the one most players will recognise, and because the game is a celebration of that kind of silhouette. Once that one is in the game and the partnership is proven, I would like to add the Fenyr SuperSport and the Sabre to make a proper three-car garage. I am seeking a **non-exclusive** licence and cannot offer exclusivity.

**What makes this easy for you.** Our garage art, lighting and camera are entirely under our control, so we can build to your brand guidelines rather than argue about them. I am happy to run a creative review with your team before delivery, and I can share a private playable build so you can judge the game in motion rather than read about it. I have also written out the exact technical contract we work to — four independent wheel pivots, separated material slots, a 30k–60k triangle budget — so your pipeline can deliver first time without a round of back-and-forth. The full specification is in the attached brief.

**Timing.** Our target release window is **[RELEASE WINDOW]**, and I have built your approval cycle into our schedule rather than assuming a turnaround.

**Commercials.** Development is self-funded by a small team. I am happy to walk through the revenue model, marketing plan and what the licence would mean for the game's visibility — I would rather earn your decision on the game itself.

If licensing the Lykan is not something you can take on, I would still be glad to know whether the Fenyr or the Sabre might be possible instead, and I would welcome any guidance on the correct process even if the answer is that it is not a fit right now.

Thank you for your time.

## 这封信和 McLaren 那封的关键差别

| | McLaren 版 | W Motors 版 |
|---|---|---|
| **开头** | 先说清「你们是代理、我可能找错桌子」 | 先说「我为什么敬你们」——W Motors 是私营公司，**人看邮件的方式和大牌公关完全不同** |
